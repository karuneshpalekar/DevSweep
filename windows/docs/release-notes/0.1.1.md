# DevSweep for Windows 0.1.1

A polish release after the first install reports.

## Fixed

- **The icon is DevSweep's logo.** The window, taskbar, Alt+Tab, the notification-area (tray) icon,
  the toast notifications, `DevSweep.exe` in Explorer, the Start Menu and desktop shortcuts, the
  installer and Apps & features all show the real logo now (they showed a generic icon, or a drawn
  placeholder).
- **Every place is in the navigation.** Home, Clean up, Projects and Health are listed on the left, with
  History and Settings at the bottom. Settings is also the gear at the top right of the window. Projects
  and Health are on by default now (they were off, so they were missing from the list); switch either
  off in Settings, Modules and its place is hidden again.
- **Settings could be pushed off the bottom of the screen** on a small or scaled display, because the
  window opened a fixed size taller than the screen. The window now fits the screen's usable area, has
  a minimum size (1000 x 680), and the navigation scrolls inside its pane instead of losing items.
- **Light and dark are easy to find.** A button at the top right of the window (next to the window
  buttons) cycles Windows setting, Light and Dark, with its current choice written on it. It is the same
  setting as Settings, General, Appearance, and the title bar, the window buttons and the content all
  follow it as you click.
- The first-run Welcome screen now says where everything is.

## Upgrading

Install over 0.1.0 (the installer upgrades in place and keeps your History, settings and quarantine).
Your saved module choices are kept: if you had switched Health or Projects on, they stay on; if you
want them off, Settings, Modules.
